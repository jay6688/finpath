# FinPath V1 novice test plan

## Research goal

Can a person with little finance knowledge figure out what to do, understand
the concepts in the intended way, and explain the important distinctions
without developer help?

This is a comprehension and navigation study, not a visual-preference survey
and not a user-facing score.

## Setup

- Use the production deployment on a normal phone first when practical.
- Start in a fresh/private browser with FinPath progress cleared.
- Record device, browser and session length.
- Say only: **“This is a financial-learning prototype. Please use it as you
  naturally would.”**
- Do not disclose the intended route unless the participant is completely
  stuck.
- Ask the participant to think aloud. If they become silent, ask: **“What are
  you thinking right now?”**

## Moderator rules

Do not teach, correct or rescue immediately. Use neutral prompts:

- “What are you looking for now?”
- “What do you think this means?”
- “What would you click next?”
- “What makes you say that?”

Avoid “Do you understand this?”, “This means...” and “Click here.” Record the
point where help became necessary and what minimum help was given.

## Tasks

### A. Start

“You want to learn how to understand a company’s financial numbers. Start
wherever you think makes sense.”

Observe whether Home leads naturally to Learn and Lesson 1.

### B. Revenue and Profit

After the relevant lessons: “In your own words, what is the difference between
Revenue and Net Income?”

### C. Profit and Cash

“If a company reports Net Income, does that mean it received exactly that much
cash?”

### D. Balance Sheet

- “Are Assets the same thing as Cash?”
- “Are Borrowings the same thing as Total Liabilities?”

### E. EPS

- “Is EPS the stock price?”
- “Why does the lesson use weighted-average shares?”

Accept plain-language understanding; do not require textbook wording.

### F. Market Cap

Let the participant use the educational calculator, then ask: “Was the price
in this lesson supplied by FinPath as real market data?”

### G. P/E

- “What two things are being compared?”
- “Does a low P/E automatically mean cheap?”

### H. Business Model

“Before reading the financial numbers, what would you want to know about how
the company makes money?”

### I. Capstone

“What can this analysis say confidently, and what can it not say yet?”

Observe whether missing valuation evidence remains unknown rather than becoming
an invented conclusion.

## Observation and coding

Track:

- task completion without moderator help;
- hesitation and repeated backtracking;
- navigation dead ends;
- wrong conceptual interpretations;
- terms that required clarification;
- where Evidence was opened spontaneously;
- where Evidence distracted from learning;
- mobile interaction friction.

After each explanation classify understanding as **understood**, **partial**,
**misunderstood**, or **not reached**. This classification is for research
notes only.

## Post-test questions

- Which lesson felt easiest?
- Which lesson was hardest?
- Where did you feel lost?
- Was there any word you did not understand?
- Did you know what to do next?
- Did source/evidence information help or distract you?
- What do you think FinPath is trying to teach you?
- What would you want to learn next?

Do not use “Did you like it?” as the main outcome.
